Take part responsibly

  1. Use fictional situations and broad locality names. Do not post personal contact details or real emergencies.
  2. Send a need, then a compatible offer from a different participant. Check that the desk suggests a connection.
  3. Ask the coordinator to review and approve it. An open simulation approval never sends a real Telegram notification.
  4. Optionally send /iam India (or your country) to contribute a self-reported country count.
  5. Send /forget to delete your stored app records after testing.

These counts are participation records, not verified people or countries. No claim of real adoption is made.
